body_default

Includes changes from DF Revised 2.2.0
Includes changes from Sver's DF Combat Reworked 1.7
Includes changes from DPh Kraken's Immaterial Cultures
Includes changes from Modest Mod

	Sver: Facial features, including teeth, are now internal; eyes are exempted from it by popular demand.
		Facial features can no longer be individually targeted or severed in combat (reduces post-siege clutter), but strikes to the head can sometimes damage them.
		This also makes them covered by helmets instead of sleeved body armor.
		Fingers and toes are now internal and can no longer be severed (reduces post-siege clutter).
		Joints have been made thicker and sturdier, including the Revised shoulders and hips in individual body plans.

 Renamed body parts
  upper body -> torso
  lower body -> lower extremities? considered groin/waist/abdomen
  lower arm  -> forearm
  upper leg  -> thigh
  lower leg  -> calf
  front leg  -> foreleg
  rear leg   -> hind leg
  neck       -> upper spine

[OBJECT:BODY]

*** Body part combat changes

Sver: Fingers, toes, teeth, lips, eyelids, noses, beaks and tongues are now INTERNAL and can no longer be severed (reduces post-siege clutter)
Kraken: anatomical names for 5-digit anatomy
Zey: added RIGHT/LEFT tokens to most bodyparts that have right/left in the name
