[STEAM_CHANGELOG:1.4  fixed custom lua scripts - they're not functional!  fixed many outstanding bugs.  procedurally generated creatures (demons, angels, forgotten beasts, titans, etc) now have tougher body materials and sapient ones can rule over civilizations and emit an aura of terror.  semimegabeasts/megabeasts have an innate resistance against damage. necromancers and most undead have a huge innate damage resistance, which can be countered by in weapons made of increasing order of potency metals; steel/silver/mithril.  implemented the mod vanilla weapon overhaul by lmlsna with permission!  fixed tool/furniture bugs associated with their materials.  halflings should now bring honey bee products in trade caravans.  removed alt human civs and mostly reset the other civs to vanilla ethics and cultural values - will return when im satisfied with their quality.  large number of worldgen stability improvements; worlds around 1000 years old should have a much more stable population.  overhauled metallurgy reactions; alloys now require a realistic amount of proportial metals and can no longer be smelted using ores. efficiency smelting reactions are reworked. reactions requiring flux now have a alternate recipes that use crushed flux - these reactions have a 15% chance to yield an additional bar/product.]
